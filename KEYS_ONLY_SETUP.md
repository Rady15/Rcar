# Keys-only production activation

The codebase is designed to remain fail-closed until real credentials are supplied.

Required secrets:
- DATABASE_URL
- AUTH_SECRET (32+ random chars)
- ADMIN_INITIAL_PASSWORD (12+ chars)
- PAYMENT_API_KEY + PAYMENT_WEBHOOK_SECRET when online payments are enabled
- TAMM_API_KEY when TAMM is enabled
- ZATCA_API_KEY and any certificate/CSID material required by the official integration
- SMS_API_KEY / SMS_PROVIDER_URL when SMS is enabled
- EMAIL_API_KEY / EMAIL_PROVIDER_URL when email is enabled
- WHATSAPP_API_KEY / WHATSAPP_PROVIDER_URL when WhatsApp is enabled
- S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID / S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY / S3_BUCKET / S3_ENDPOINT for private document storage

Non-secret public configuration:
- APP_URL
- PAYMENT_API_URL
- TAMM_API_URL
- ZATCA_API_URL
- S3_REGION

No fake credentials, fake payment success, fake government authorization, or fake invoice clearance is generated by the application.
